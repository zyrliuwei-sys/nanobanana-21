Nano Banana 2.1 is the popular name for **gemini-nano-banana-2.1**, Google's newest lightweight image generation and editing model in the Gemini 3 family. It sits in the Flash tier, which means it is tuned for speed and low cost, yet it produces images that hold up at full 4K resolution.

This guide covers what the model does, how it differs from earlier versions and how to start using it on nano banana 2.1 without an API key.

## What changed in Nano Banana 2.1

Nano Banana 2.1 follows Nano Banana 2 and Nano Banana Pro. Instead of adding novelty features, the update focuses on the problems people run into in real work:

- **Visual quality at 1K, 2K and 4K.** Skin tones, material detail, lighting and backgrounds look more natural and more consistent.
- **No tiling on wide frames.** Wide and panoramic aspect ratios at 2K and 4K no longer show repeating seams.
- **Better text and infographics.** Signs, posters, charts and maps come out with correct spelling and cleaner layout.
- **Character consistency.** The same person or mascot stays recognizable across multiple images and edits.
- **Speed.** It is reported to be close to 3x faster than the previous Flash image model.

## Nano Banana 2.1 specs

- **Model ID:** `gemini-nano-banana-2.1`
- **Family:** Gemini 3, Flash tier
- **Output:** 1K, 2K or 4K
- **Reference images:** Up to 14 per request
- **Aspect ratios:** 1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 3:4, 3:2, 2:3, 5:4, 4:5, 21:9, 4:1, 1:4, 8:1, 1:8
- **Input:** Text and images

## What you can do with it

**Text to image.** Describe a scene in plain language and get a finished image. Mention the subject first, then setting, lighting and style.

**Precise local edits.** Upload a photo and describe one change, such as a new wall color or a removed object. The rest of the image stays as it was.

**Product recontextualization.** Place a product photo into a new scene while its shape, label and finish stay true to the original. This is useful for e-commerce listings and ads.

**Multi-image fusion.** Combine up to 14 reference images in one request, for example a model, a jacket and a street scene merged into one photo.

**Readable text.** Put the exact wording in quotes and say where it should appear. Nano Banana 2.1 handles posters, menus, labels and diagrams far better than earlier models.

## How to use Nano Banana 2.1 online

1. Open the [generator on the homepage](/#create) and sign in. New accounts receive free credits.
2. Write a prompt, or switch to _Edit an image_, _Multi-image fusion_ or _Product shot_ and upload your references.
3. Choose an aspect ratio and a resolution: 1K, 2K or 4K.
4. Click **Generate**. When the result appears, download it or click **Edit this** to keep refining it.

Every image costs credits based on resolution, and failed generations are refunded automatically. See [pricing](/pricing) for packs and plans.
