Nano Banana 2.1 follows instructions closely, so a clear prompt gets you most of the way. These rules and examples work in the [nano banana 2.1 generator](/#create); copy any prompt and adjust it.

## 1. Lead with the subject

Say what the picture is about before anything else, then add setting, light and style. Long lists of adjectives help less than one concrete scene.

> Editorial portrait of a young woman with freckles in golden hour light, natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, shot on 85mm

## 2. Describe light like a photographer

Name the light source and its quality: window light, rim light, overcast, neon reflections. Nano Banana 2.1 is strong at realistic lighting when you are specific.

> Luxury perfume bottle standing on wet black volcanic rocks, lime green rim light, water droplets, studio product photography

## 3. Quote the exact text

Text rendering is one of the biggest upgrades in 2.1. Put the wording in quotes and say where it belongs.

> A small coffee shop window at dusk with a hand-painted sign that reads "OPEN LATE" in cream letters, warm interior light

## 4. Pick the ratio for the destination

Choose the aspect ratio in the generator rather than describing it in the prompt: 9:16 for stories, 4:5 for feed posts, 16:9 or 21:9 for banners and key visuals.

## 5. Name your references by order

With several uploads, refer to them as image 1, image 2 and so on, and give each one a role.

> The model from image 1 wearing the jacket from image 2, standing in the street from image 3, overcast afternoon light

## 6. Keep products true

For product shots, say what must not change, then describe the new scene.

> Keep the bottle shape, label and color exactly the same. Place it on a marble kitchen counter with soft morning light and a sprig of rosemary

## 7. Edit one thing at a time

Change a single element per request, then click **Edit this** on the result and continue. Small steps keep faces, products and layouts stable.

> Repaint the back wall sage green. Keep the sofa, lighting and camera angle unchanged

## 8. Use 1K to explore, 4K to finish

Draft ideas at 1K (10 credits), then regenerate the winner at 2K or 4K for print or zoom. The prompt stays the same; only the resolution changes.
